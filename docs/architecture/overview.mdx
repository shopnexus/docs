---
title: "Architecture Overview"
description: "How ShopNexus is structured as a set of cooperating services."
---

ShopNexus follows a **service-oriented architecture**: business capabilities are split into
independent services, each owning its data and exposing a contract that other services and
clients consume.

## High-level shape

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Client[Web / Mobile Client] --> GW[API Gateway]
  GW --> S1[Service A]
  GW --> S2[Service B]
  GW --> S3[Service C]
  S1 <--> MB[(Message Broker)]
  S2 <--> MB
  S3 <--> MB
  S1 --> DB1[(DB A)]
  S2 --> DB2[(DB B)]
  S3 --> DB3[(DB C)]
```
