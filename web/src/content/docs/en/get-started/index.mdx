ShopNexus is a marketplace where people in Vietnam sell used things to each other. Two constraints shape everything else: the platform holds nobody's money, and it does not judge disputes.

A deal has one path, <Term id="cod-inspection">COD đồng kiểm</Term>. The buyer looks at the item at the door and only then pays the shipper. The seller links their own GHTK or GHN account once, and ShopNexus creates each waybill on that account. The carrier pays the COD money straight to the seller, and ShopNexus never handles it.

Trust is a set of facts people see before they deal: ratings both ways, items sold, how soon a seller answers. Staff step in only for banned or fake goods, organised scams, requests from the authorities and support tickets.

People sign in with a phone number that Firebase verifies, a Google account or a code sent by email. Posting a listing, opening a deal and opening a chat need a phone number.

## The pieces

- A Go server on Postgres (ParadeDB), Restate and RustFS, with an OpenAPI contract. Every model call goes through Bifrost, a gateway in each stack.
- A native Android app and a website for buyers and sellers.
- A web console for staff, with an AI agent that answers their questions from the records.
