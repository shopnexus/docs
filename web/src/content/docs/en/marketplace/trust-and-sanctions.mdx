Since the platform judges no case, trust is a set of facts people can see before they deal. There are no points, tiers or badges to earn or buy.

## What people see

On a listing and on a seller's page:

- The rating as a seller: the average stars and the number of reviews.
- How many items the seller has sold, in the app or marked sold by hand.
- How soon the seller usually answers, once they have answered at least 5 people in the last 30 days.
- Whether the seller's phone number is verified. The number itself is never shown.

When a buyer makes an offer, the seller sees the buyer's own rating as a buyer.

## Reviews

After an order is completed or failed, the buyer and the seller review each other once. A review stays hidden until both have written one, or until 7 days after the order ended, so neither side answers the other's stars. While it is hidden its writer can change or delete it. The person reviewed can reply once, and then the review is final.

## Your reputation

**Uy tín của bạn** shows each person their own figures:

- Their rating as a seller and as a buyer.
- Items sold, followers and when they joined.
- Their reply rate: how often they answered within 24 hours when someone wrote about their listings, once at least 5 such chats count.
- As a buyer, the COD orders of the last 90 days and how many failed.

## Limits for everyone

Every account has the same limits. A paid seller plan cannot raise them.

- At most 20 listings at once, counting those on sale, being processed, hidden, refused or reserved for an order.
- At most 3 open COD orders as a buyer, requested or on their way.

When a buyer refuses a parcel, the seller pays shipping both ways. The limit of 3 open orders keeps that risk small for sellers.

## Scam warnings

ShopNexus reads chats for common scam signs, such as a request to pay first, to move off the app, a strange link, a request for an OTP or card number, or a fake payment proof. When the other person shows one, the app warns you under their message. It may add that their account is new or the price is far below similar listings. Phone numbers, bank numbers, emails and links are hidden before the check reads the chat.

You can dismiss a warning. It comes back only if a new sign appears. The person the warning is about never sees it.

## Sanctions

Nothing is sanctioned automatically. Staff act on reports, and only for banned or fake goods, organised scams and requests from the authorities (see [Moderation](/en/marketplace/moderation)). They can:

- Hide a listing. The seller cannot show it again until staff lift it.
- Suspend an account, with a reason and possibly an end date.

A suspended account cannot sign in again or write anything new: no listings, messages, offers, COD requests, reviews, reports, follows or profile changes. It can still withdraw an offer, decline, cancel an order already under way, and open or answer a support ticket.

A sanction removes rights only, since the platform holds no money to fine anyone with.
