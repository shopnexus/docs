Staff handle three kinds of case:

- Reported listings of prohibited or counterfeit goods.
- Scams that show signs of being organised.
- Valid requests from the authorities.

The number of these cases does not grow with the number of orders. Everything else, from a refused parcel to a seller who declines an issue report, goes into the statistics described in [Trust and sanctions](/en/marketplace/trust-and-sanctions). A seller who lies about a shipment is reported as a user, the same way.

Moderators and admins work in a separate web console, on staff accounts of their own. They can suspend a customer account with a reason and an end date, and every such action is kept in an audit log.

User reports are part of the design and are not built on the server yet.
