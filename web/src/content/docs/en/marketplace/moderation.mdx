Staff handle only these cases:

- Reported listings of banned or counterfeit goods.
- Scams that show signs of being organised.
- Valid requests from the authorities.
- Support tickets.

The number of these cases does not grow with the number of orders. Price and condition are between the buyer and the seller. A refused parcel or a slow answer goes into the [figures](/en/marketplace/trust-and-sanctions) people see, not into a case.

## Reporting

Anyone signed in can report a listing or an account, with a reason (banned goods, fake, scam or other) and a short note. A report of an account can include the chat with that person, so staff can read it. To stop someone writing while you report them, block them first; the chat stays for the report.

You have one open report per listing or account. Reporting it again updates your report. You can undo a report, and you can see your reports and whether each is still open or resolved.

## How staff handle reports

Staff work in a separate web console, on accounts of their own. The reports about one person form one case. A staff member reads the case, then either dismisses it as no violation or acts on it: hides a listing, or suspends the account with a reason and, if it is temporary, an end date. A closed case can be reopened, and the next report about the person opens a new case.

The console's AI assistant can read a case and answer questions about it, but only a staff member makes a change. Every action is kept in an audit log.
