A <Term id="boost">boost</Term> (đẩy tin) is a package a seller buys for one of their listings on sale (<Req id="UC-LST-03" />). It moves the listing to the top of the newest listings as soon as it is paid, then again every 24 hours until the package ends.

| Package | Price | Moves up |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days | 15.000 ₫ | 3 times |
| 7 days | 29.000 ₫ | 7 times |

<Req id="BR-LST-04" />

## What moves

Only lists sorted by newest change order: search and category pages sorted newest, Home's For you and Recently viewed, and the seller's profile. Relevance, nearby and price sorts stay as they are. The card keeps its posted time and shows **Được đẩy**. A bump is not a new listing, so it sends nothing to people who watch a search or follow the seller.

## Who can buy

Only the seller, for a listing on sale, while their account may write and has a phone. A listing has one package at a time: buying again while one runs, or another package while one awaits payment, is refused (<Req id="BR-LST-05" />). The app offers **Đẩy tin** only when a purchase would go through.

## Paying

The seller pays on VNPay's page, once per purchase. ShopNexus keeps no balance or wallet (<Req id="ADR-0006" />). The package starts when VNPay confirms the payment to ShopNexus, never on the strength of the page the browser comes back to, and its days count from then. An unpaid purchase lapses after 15 minutes, and VNPay's page takes no payment after that. One the seller cancels on that page closes at once. Either way the seller is told and nothing is charged.

This money is the seller's payment to ShopNexus for a service. It has nothing to do with a deal: buyers and sellers still pay each other only at the door (<Req id="BR-DEAL-01" />).

## No refunds

ShopNexus refunds nothing, for a deal or for a boost (<Req id="BR-LST-07" />). Money that arrives always starts the package, even if the listing was hidden or reserved at that moment.

A listing hidden, reserved, held by staff or no longer on sale on a bump day is not moved that day, and the package gets no extra day (<Req id="BR-LST-06" />).

The seller gets a notice when a package starts, ends, lapses or fails.
