ShopNexus takes no fee per order. Its revenue is what a seller buys for their own listings.

- A <Term id="boost">boost</Term> (đẩy tin) moves a listing to the top of the newest order at once and again every 24 hours: 3 days for 15.000 ₫ or 7 days for 29.000 ₫ (<Req id="BR-LST-04" />). The seller pays once per purchase through VNPay; there is no wallet. How it works is in [Boosts](/en/marketplace/boosts).
- A seller plan, not built. The free plan would be Cơ bản. Plus would cost 49.000 ₫ a month and include 4 boosts a month, used before any paid boost, renew monthly and run to the end of the paid month when cancelled.

Neither buys trust. A plan cannot raise a seller's limits or change what buyers see about them.

## No fee per order

The waybill sits on the seller's own carrier account, so ShopNexus collects no shipping fee. The platform never touches the price either, which leaves it no practical way to take a percentage of each order.

Whether carriers pay a commission to a marketplace that brings shops to them is an open question for GHTK and GHN.
