ShopNexus takes no fee per order. The planned revenue is two things the seller buys, and neither is built yet.

- A <Term id="boost">boost</Term> (đẩy tin) would put a listing at the top of the nearby feed for one or more days. The app's design prices it at 15.000 ₫ for one day and 35.000 ₫ for three.
- A seller plan. The free plan is Cơ bản. Plus would cost 49.000 ₫ a month and include 4 boosts a month, used before any paid boost. A plan would renew monthly and run to the end of the paid month when cancelled.

Neither buys trust. A plan cannot raise a seller's limits or change what buyers see about them.

## No fee per order

The waybill sits on the seller's own carrier account, so ShopNexus collects no shipping fee. The platform never touches the price either, which leaves it no practical way to take a percentage of each order.

Whether carriers pay a commission to a marketplace that brings shops to them is an open question for GHTK and GHN.
