Revenue comes from two things the seller buys.

- A <Term id="boost">boost</Term> (đẩy tin) puts a listing at the top of the nearby feed for one or more days. The app's design prices it at 15.000 ₫ for one day and 35.000 ₫ for three.
- A seller plan. The free plan is Cơ bản. Plus costs 49.000 ₫ a month and includes 4 boosts a month, which are used before any paid boost. A plan renews monthly and runs to the end of the paid month when cancelled.

Neither buys trust. A plan cannot buy COD rights or a reputation tier.

## No fee per order

The waybill sits on the seller's own carrier account, so ShopNexus collects no shipping fee. The platform never touches the price either, which leaves it no practical way to take a percentage of each order.

Whether carriers pay a commission to a marketplace that refers shops to them is an open question for GHTK and GHN.

Billing is planned as its own server module and is not built yet, so the prices above come from the app's design.
