Money changes hands only on events both sides can see: the shipper at the door, the COD money reaching the seller's bank, the parcel coming back. The buyer pays nothing in the app. There is no deposit, wallet or credit, so the platform never owes anyone a refund.

## Shipping

The waybill is set so the recipient pays the shipping. At the door, after looking at the item, the buyer pays the shipper the price and the outbound fee together. ShopNexus takes no part of the fee, because the waybill sits on the seller's own carrier account.

ShopNexus sets no shipping deadline, because the carrier's COD system already has one.

## Cancelling

Either the buyer or the seller can cancel an order until the seller records the waybill. Nobody has paid anything at that point. Once the waybill is in, the order can no longer be cancelled, so a buyer cannot cancel on a seller who has already handed the parcel to the carrier.

## Refusing at the door

Refusing the parcel is the real refund. The buyer who refuses before paying loses nothing, and the parcel goes back to the seller. The carrier charges the outbound and the return shipping to the seller's account.

<Callout type="warning">
After the buyer has paid the shipper, ShopNexus has no way to get the money back. The app says this before the buyer pays. If the item turns out to have a problem, the buyer opens an issue report in the chat (see [After delivery](/en/marketplace/after-delivery)).
</Callout>

A licensed escrow option for expensive categories is kept for later and is not built.
