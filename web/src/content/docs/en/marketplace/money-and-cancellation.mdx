Money changes hands only on events both sides can see: the shipper at the door, the COD money reaching the seller, the parcel coming back. The buyer pays nothing in the app, so the platform never owes anyone a refund.

## Shipping

ShopNexus creates the waybill on the seller's own carrier account, set so the recipient pays the shipping. At the door, after looking at the item, the buyer pays the shipper the price and the shipping fee together. The order shows the fee as soon as the carrier tells it. ShopNexus takes no part of the fee.

## Cancelling

Either the buyer or the seller can cancel an order until the seller ships it. Nobody has paid anything at that point. Once the seller has shipped, neither side can cancel, so a buyer cannot cancel on a seller who has already handed the parcel over.

Two deadlines cancel an order on their own: a seller who does not ship within 3 days, and a parcel the carrier has not picked up 3 days after the seller shipped. The [COD deal](/en/marketplace/cod-deal#deadlines) lists them.

## Refusing at the door

Refusing the parcel is the real refund. The buyer who refuses before paying loses nothing, and the parcel goes back to the seller. The carrier charges the outbound and the return shipping to the seller's account.

<Callout type="warning">
After the buyer has paid the shipper, ShopNexus has no way to get the money back. The app says this before the buyer pays. Any refund after that is between the two people, by their own transfer (see [After delivery](/en/marketplace/after-delivery)).
</Callout>
