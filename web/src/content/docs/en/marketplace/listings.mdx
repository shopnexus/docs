A listing is one item at one price, sold by one person to another and paid for on delivery. It is a set of commitments the buyer can check at the door, written as fields instead of a block of free text.

## What a listing commits to

- A condition on a fixed scale: Mới (new), Như mới (like new) or Đã dùng (used).
- <Term id="listing-claims">Tin ghi</Term>, the facts the seller states, such as "Pin 88%", "Máy zin, chưa sửa" or "Bản VN/A". At the door the buyer can open them with a note on how to check each one.
- Photos. A photo taken with the in-app camera carries its capture time. Photos from the library are allowed, and the form counts them ("2/3 ảnh chụp trong app").
- A price in whole đồng, from 10.000 ₫ to 1.000.000.000 ₫. The ceiling catches a price typed with extra zeros.

## Help from the model

The seller starts from photos or a short video, and the model fills in the title, category, specs and description. A video gives it more to work with, since the seller can say the battery level or the price out loud. Whatever the seller says in the video stays in the description, scratches included, because those are the seller's own words.

The draft stays on the phone until it is complete. A new listing goes through photo processing and then becomes public.

## No declared marks

A listing has no section where the seller declares marks, and the model never points out a scratch or dent. People judge wear on used goods differently, so a declared mark is not a reliable fact. A mark named by the model would also give a buyer grounds to complain after the sale, which drives sellers away. The buyer sees the real condition at the door, before paying. This was decided on 2026-10-27.

## Limits

A seller can have up to 20 listings at once that are processing, active, hidden or rejected. While a parcel for a listing is on its way, the listing is reserved: other buyers no longer find it in search, and the seller cannot edit, hide or delete it.
