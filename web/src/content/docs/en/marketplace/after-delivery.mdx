ShopNexus records what happened after a sale and does not rule on it. At the scale of one case nobody can tell who is right, and the platform holds no money it could move anyway. The buyer's protection is the inspection at the door: they pay only after looking at the item, and refusing costs them nothing.

## Reviews

Once an order is completed or failed, the buyer and the seller review each other, once each: 1 to 5 stars and up to 500 characters. A review rates the person, as a seller or as a buyer.

- A review stays hidden until the other side has also reviewed, or 7 days after the order closed, so neither side writes in answer to the other.
- The writer can edit or delete it for 7 days after first posting it. Deleting and posting again does not restart the 7 days.
- The person reviewed can reply once, up to 500 characters, to a visible review. A reply locks the review.

## A parcel that never arrived

When the carrier reports a parcel delivered but the buyer never got it, the buyer opens a **Báo chưa nhận hàng** ticket from the order. It is open while the order is shipped, or for 7 days after the order completed on its own. There is one such ticket per order, and staff answer it in the ticket's chat.

## After paying

Once the buyer has paid the shipper, ShopNexus has no way to get the money back. Any refund is between the two people, by their own transfer.
