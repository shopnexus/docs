Deals Deals and orders

Make an offer, or a counter to the buyer's offer

POST/v1/deals/{id}/offer
UC-DEAL-02 · Negotiate a price Bearer token Safe to retry: no

The buyer offers; the seller counters the buyer's open offer. The amount must lie inside the deal's offer_range.

Path 1 parameter

idstring · uuidrequired

The deal.

Body application/json · Offer

amountinteger · int64required

Whole đồng in multiples of 1,000, inside the deal's offer_range. The range is a business rule, so an amount outside it is 422.

Responses with the error codes each one returns

200The deal, with the new open price.
400The body does not match the spec.invalid_request
401No valid access token.unauthenticatedsession_expired
403The account is suspended.account_suspended
404No such deal of the caller's.deal_not_found
409The deal or the listing does not allow it now.move_not_allowedlisting_unavailabledeal_changed
422The amount breaks the offer range.invalid_amount
