# Website: the Tươi look, then the follow-ups

Goal (2026-11-26): build the Tươi desktop drafts (`website/.superdesign/mockups/tuoi-web-*.html`, builders
`tools/build_tuoi_web*.py`) into the Astro site, then fix the mismatches found while drawing them.

Work in `website/.worktrees/tuoi` on `feat/tuoi`. Each phase: implement, gate, review, merge into local `main`
(no push).

## Ground rules

- The app is the source: `app/core/designsystem/.../theme` (Color.kt, Palette.kt, Lift.kt, Type.kt, Shape.kt). The web
  copies its colour roles (light and dark), palette and lift, and keeps its type a step up.
- The utilities stay semantic (`bg-card`, `text-sub`, `rounded-card`…). Change what they mean first, so every
  page moves at once. Then redraw each page's layout to match its draft.
- Compact (< 600) keeps the bottom bar and the app's phone layout. The drafts are desktop (1440 and 1366×657).
- Copy comes from the existing pages and i18n. A draft only changes layout, unless it fixes copy that
  code already has.
- Icons are MingCute (`@iconify-json/mingcute`). The one map from Material Symbols names to MingCute names is also the
  source for the app's `symbols.txt`.

## Phases

1. **Foundation.** `tokens.css`: Tươi roles (light and dark), palette utilities (sun, sun-soft, sky, mint, lemon,
   sage, aqua, sand), `shadow-lift` (the app's two layers), card radius 24, field radius 18, control radius 16, type a
   step up with the app's weights. `j.css`: K2 greys become Tươi values. A `card` utility (white, lift, 24 px, no
   hairline). Icons switch to MingCute. Update DESIGN.md's direction section.
2. **Shell.** Header (logo, Danh mục, search with camera, bell, chat, avatar, Đăng tin), footer, account nav
   (Mua / Bán / Tài khoản, with Chợ Tốt and Quyền riêng tư), listing card, buttons, chips, tags, fields, step bar.
3. **Core pages.** Home (signed in and guest), search, listing, messages, seller, saved, Tôi, orders, sell.
4. **The three flows.**
   - COD setup: intro, address, carrier, GHTK and GHN linking, done, Giao COD.
   - Tạo đơn, Đơn bán, and the order pages for seller and buyer.
   - Chợ Tốt linking: new pages on `/v1/me/chotot`.
5. **The rest.** Categories, category, 404, help, guides, legal, account pages, notifications, plans, sign-in,
   support, peeks, quick view, buy, photo search, sell preview.
6. **Follow-ups.**
   - Carrier preselection: settle GHTK against GHN.
   - The app's "Đã tạo đơn GHTK" snackbar ignores the carrier.
   - The server ignores the GHN hand-over choice.
   - The website does not require COD before posting.
   - Help's hard-coded "2 phiếu".
   - The app's `design-system.md` is out of date.
   - The app's icons move to MingCute.

## Gate per phase

- `bunx astro check` and `bun run build`.
- Astro audit (`~/shopnexus/tmp/astro-audit/run.mjs`): desktop clean.
- Playwright screenshots of the touched routes at 1440×900, 1366×657 and 390×844 against the dev API, compared with
  the drafts.
- `/code-review`, then `/simplify`.
- Merge into `main` from a temporary worktree if `main` is checked out elsewhere.
